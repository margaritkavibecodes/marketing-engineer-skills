# Product launch kit

Order matters. Tracking before ads. Conversions checked before tracking counts as done. Profiles
live before your schema points at them. Work top to bottom. Each step says what "done" means, so
you check it instead of believing it.

## 1. Site and tracking

- One tag manager container. Analytics runs inside it.
- The consent banner loads first, and tags fire only after consent.
- One analytics property per product. Search console verified, sitemap submitted.
- A weekly site audit. Most warnings on a new site are noise; read the list before you panic.
- Done when you load the page in a clean browser, accept cookies, and see the visit arrive.

## 2. Conversions on real actions

- List the actions that mean someone wants to buy. Fire each one on the action itself.
- Check what each conversion really fires on. A "download" that fires on a page view is a page view.
- Trigger every conversion yourself once, then mark it in analytics and the ad platforms.
- Keep them separate. Never add different actions together in a report.
- Done when you've seen every conversion arrive in analytics and in the ad platform.

## 3. Search ads on intent

- Keywords people type when they have the problem now.
- A small budget first. Raise it only after the search terms show the right people.
- Negative keywords from week one.

## 4. Profiles and entity

- A company page and a video channel, even with one video.
- Organization schema with `sameAs` listing every profile that is live.
- An entity in Wikidata. An `llms.txt` at the site root.
- The same name everywhere.

## 5. Legal pages

- Privacy and cookie notices in plain language, in every language the site has.
- Company details copied from the official register.
- Say what actually runs on the site. If you use remarketing, say so.

## 6. Lead magnet

- Something your buyer would print and forward: a guide to the problem, not a brochure about you.
- Every fact sourced. One call to action, at the end.
- Separate tracking for the download and any printed copy, so you know which one works.

## 7. Switch on the routines

- `taste-rules` first, before anything writes a word.
- `seo-geo-loop`: the weekly radar and writer.
- `ads-watchdog`: the Monday report with your goals file.
- A first round of AI citation checks as your baseline.
- One repo per workflow. The agents push, you pull.
